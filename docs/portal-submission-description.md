## Week 6 Internship Report: Performance Evaluation and Continuous Improvement in SAP SCM

This Week 6 report presents a Performance Evaluation and Continuous Improvement Analysis in SAP SCM using a six-month simulated supply-chain scenario for the fictional Deccan Harvest Foods Pvt. Ltd., a manufacturer of millet-based ready-to-cook products. The objective is to evaluate operational performance through a balanced set of key performance indicators (KPIs), identify persistent bottlenecks, and propose actions that can be monitored and validated over time. The report explains how KPI reporting, planning books and key figures, the SAP APO Plan Monitor, and threshold-based alerts where SAP Integrated Business Planning is deployed can support performance review. The actual reporting tools must be selected according to the organization's SAP release and system architecture.

The assessment includes simulated monthly data from April to September 2026 for customer on-time-in-full (OTIF), forecast accuracy, production schedule adherence, supplier OTIF, inventory days of supply, logistics cost per shipped tonne, critical-exception resolution time, packaging-material shortage incidents, resource utilization, scrap/expiry, data reconciliation accuracy, demand tonnes, and shipped tonnes. The simulated results show customer OTIF improving from 86.0% to 93.0%, forecast accuracy from 81.0% to 88.5%, production schedule adherence from 82.0% to 91.0%, and supplier OTIF from 78.0% to 89.0%. Inventory cover decreases from 34 to 27 days, modeled logistics cost falls from ₹6,800 to ₹6,150 per tonne, and median critical-exception resolution falls from 16 to 6 hours.

Although the trends improve, September still has gaps against proposed targets: customer OTIF remains below 95%, supplier OTIF below 95%, exception resolution above four hours, packaging shortages above two incidents per month, and scrap/expiry above 2.5%. The report connects these results to prioritized countermeasures, including supplier reliability review, SKU/location-level forecast analysis, finite-capacity schedule controls, exception ownership, inventory parameter validation, shelf-life and quality controls, and end-to-end data reconciliation.

The submission includes KPI definitions and formulas, a simulated dashboard and trend charts, root-cause hypotheses, a Plan-Do-Check-Act improvement cycle, governance responsibilities, risk controls, twelve proposed validation tests, and a practical 30/60/90-day roadmap. All figures, targets, trends, dashboard graphics, and benefit estimates are illustrative learning assumptions rather than verified historical results. The report is a performance-evaluation and configuration blueprint and does not claim that the KPIs were extracted from or the tests executed in a live SAP production system.
